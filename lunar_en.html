Lunar Phase ‧ Practice Guide

This is a mirror reflecting your spiritual state. On the 1st of each month, the system condenses your interactions across 'Bodhicitta, Vows, and Prostration', presenting your monthly feedback.

Three Karmas & Faith, Vows, Action

Your daily practice is a profound introspection of Precepts, Concentration, and Wisdom:

Bodhicitta: Ignite the Bodhi mind, establish precepts, deepening the root of 'Faith'.
Vows: Practice Samantabhadra's vows, dwell in concentration, guiding the direction of 'Vows'.
Prostration: Sincere bowing to Amitabha, deepen wisdom, grounding the steps of 'Action'.

Integrating the Six Paramitas

Starting from these three karmas, as we face situations in life, we internally realize the Four Noble Truths and externally practice the Eightfold Path. Through these interactions, we naturally integrate giving, precepts, patience, diligence, meditation, and wisdom into our daily lives.
